Privacy Policy

SMASH IT (Android, package com.smashit.game) · Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information SMASH IT ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by 55Games, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). Questions: support55games@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You do not create an account and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • The game shows ads. Ad partners use your device's advertising ID and technical data to serve and measure ads.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.

Advertising

The game uses AppLovin MAX to show ads. AppLovin may request ads from partner networks, which can include Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Pangle, ironSource and others. These companies process data under their own privacy policies. See AppLovin's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time from the Settings screen. Elsewhere you can limit personalised ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Residents of US states with privacy laws can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information the same way, or by emailing us.

Analytics and crash reporting

Firebase is provided by Google. See Firebase privacy and security. The data is linked to a random app instance ID, not to your name.

Purchases

In-game purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We receive only what we need to deliver the item. Removing ads by purchase stops interstitial and banner ads; optional rewarded ads stay available.

Data on your device

Your level, coins, boosters and settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Children

SMASH IT is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, email us and we will delete what we can identify.

How long data is kept

Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies. Emails are kept as long as needed to answer you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or US state laws) you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. Because we do not hold your name, tell us your phone model and roughly when you played so we can find your data. Email support55games@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

Security and transfers

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections. Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
