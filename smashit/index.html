Support

Bug, purchase problem or question? Email support55games@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened. We answer within a few days.

  • Bought something and did not get it? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore. If it is still missing, email us your Google Play order number (starts with GPA.).
  • Lost progress? Progress is saved on your phone, so uninstalling or clearing app data resets it.
  • Ad privacy choices: Settings > Consent in the game (where available).
